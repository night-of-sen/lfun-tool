🎨 Color Converter

Convert between HEX, RGB and HSL, and generate a shade scale.

🔒 Everything runs locally in your browser. Files and content are never uploaded.

About this tool

输入任意一种格式都会实时换算成另外两种，并给出从 5% 到 95% 的明度色阶，方便挑配色。
