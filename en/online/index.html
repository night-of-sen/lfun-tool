Online tools

Small tools that run right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

🧩JSON FormatterFormat, minify and validate JSON with error positions. Runs entirely in your browser. 🔐Base64 Encoder / DecoderConvert text to and from Base64, with proper UTF-8 handling for CJK and emoji. 🔑Hash GeneratorCompute SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512 hashes locally. ⏱️Timestamp ConverterConvert between Unix timestamps and human-readable dates. 🖼️Image CompressorCompress images in the browser. Adjustable quality and size; files never leave your device. 🎨Color ConverterConvert between HEX, RGB and HSL, and generate a shade scale. 🔤Case ConverterConvert between camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and more. 🔒Password GeneratorGenerate strong passwords using the system CSPRNG. Batch mode included. 🔗URL Encoder / DecoderEncode and decode URLs, with separate modes for full URLs and component values. 📋Text DiffCompare two blocks of text line by line, highlighting additions and removals.

🔒 Everything runs locally in your browser. Files and content are never uploaded.
