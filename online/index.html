Online tools

Small tools that run right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

🧩JSON FormatterFormat, minify and validate JSON with error positions. Runs entirely in your browser. 🔐Base64 Encoder / DecoderConvert text to and from Base64, with proper UTF-8 handling for CJK and emoji. 🔑Hash GeneratorCompute SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512 hashes locally. ⏱️Timestamp ConverterConvert between Unix timestamps and human-readable dates. 🖼️Image CompressorCompress images in the browser. Adjustable quality and size; files never leave your device. 🎨Color ConverterConvert between HEX, RGB and HSL, and generate a shade scale. 🔤Case ConverterConvert between camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and more. 🔒Password GeneratorGenerate strong passwords using the system CSPRNG. Batch mode included. 🔗URL Encoder / DecoderEncode and decode URLs, with separate modes for full URLs and component values. 📋Text DiffCompare two blocks of text line by line, highlighting additions and removals. 📝Markdown PreviewWrite Markdown on the left, see it rendered on the right. Tables, code blocks and task lists included. 🎯Regex TesterTest regular expressions live, with match highlighting and capture groups. 🔢Word & Character CountCount characters, CJK characters, words and lines, with a reading-time estimate. 🧹Text Batch ToolsDeduplicate, sort, drop empty lines, trim whitespace and shuffle lines. 🎫JWT DecoderDecode a JWT header and payload, with expiry check. 🔟Number Base ConverterConvert between binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal. 🔣Escape ToolsEscape and unescape HTML entities, Unicode sequences and JSON strings. 📊CSV to JSONConvert between CSV and JSON, handling quoted fields and embedded newlines. 🔄Image ConverterConvert images to WebP, JPEG or PNG in your browser, with optional resizing. ⏰Cron Expression ExplainerTurns a cron expression into plain language and lists the next run times.
